## Position validity is not area conditioning or geometric fidelity

Simulate binary32 export followed by exact binary64 promotion and the audited
Trimesh 5.1.0 eight-digit keys:
`np.round(stored32.astype(float64)*1e8).astype(int64)`.
Binary64 multiplication rounding is part of this operation; do not replace it
with mathematically exact decimal scaling or `std::round`'s away-from-zero ties.
Reject undefined nonfinite/out-of-int64 conversions before casting.
Representative selection uses the first original referenced vertex, no snapping.
Signed zero is an exact numeric seam; its bits remain diagnostically visible.

Exact dyadic `cpp_int` predicates require noncollinearity and strictly positive
old/new normal dot on every surviving local face. New nonexact position/key
collision pairs, by original IDs globally including cross-shell pairs, veto a
transaction before commit; existing pairs may disappear, not new ones appear.
Native preflight may report active but colliding geometry as unsafe. Identity
requires safety; a future QEM branch may resolve existing pairs through legal
collapses only, never snapping/merging/repair. Nonfinite/range-invalid/inactive
F32 sources fail outright. This is a prospective general rule,
not permission to rescue source geometry by shape/scale/threshold tuning.
Future simplification must retain the native cost, placement, floating-point
intersection guard and original ±5% signed shell-volume rule; if admissibility
requires continuing below4096, queue exhaustion is failure, not valid output.
Exact predicates do not prove exact constructions or embedding.

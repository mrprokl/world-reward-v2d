# Prospective TRAIN/FIT + VAL/CAL population census seam

This new **metadata-only** seam has never measured native TRAIN capacity, accessed
Azure, acquired RGB or qualified a learned model. The earlier eight-DEV proposal
capacity result is not enough to qualify 17 parameters. This is a new population
definition, not a retry, replacement or rescue of a closed study.

`census_population(cfg, excluded, *, roles, minimum_photos, checkpoint)` returns a
scalar report and a **separate private identity inventory**. The caller must
freeze `roles={train: data__vcoco__vcoco_train.json, val:
data__vcoco__vcoco_val.json}` and `minimum_photos={train:32,val:16}`. No implicit
use/change of the original config's VAL/TEST roles or global variables occurs.
All five official split files are consulted structurally; TEST **role values**
are never opened. Missing/unknown roles are not OFF or negative evidence.

The original COCO2014 archive catalogue/tail, full inflated SHA/CRC and taxonomy
are checked through unchanged catalog/member-stream helpers. Two original
instance members are streamed once for publisher image/license metadata and
once for annotations: all selected-image instances stay in the parser context,
including crowd, invalid-area, background and unscorable role endpoints.
Annotation values for excluded photos or other populations are lexically
skipped even when `image_id` follows geometry. Each TRAIN/VAL role file is pinned
before **each** of its two field-order-independent projection passes, and its
structural image-ID union must match the native split. Native IDs, role-major
alignment, duplicate-action pair weighting and raw unclipped geometry stay
unchanged. Eligibility remains ≥2 noncrowd positive-area persons, ≥2 nonperson
objects and ≥1 localized published positive role pair; two positive agents is
diagnostic only. Duplicate fresh photo aliases are all excluded, not repaired.

The helper does not publish, select, authenticate complete execution sources,
validate historical provenance or choose hyperparameters. A future caller must
authenticate the original config/source and all metadata assets before/after,
then supply all **432 historical slots plus all16 pilot slots** (including
unavailable and reserved) as publisher photo exclusions. Known historical/pilot
MD5 aliases must also be excluded from future original-byte acquisition; absence
of a source MD5 is not a disjointness proof. Pilot reference files remain
hash-only: their role/instance values are not opened. Caller-provided checkpoint
and resource limits bind the original streaming work. No new budget is frozen
or native runtime qualified by this pure implementation.

Inventory rows contain only `{split,image_id,photo_id}` and a digest binds their
full deterministic order. They contain no boxes, actions, endpoints, labels,
predictions, RGB or selected slots and must never be mounted into inference.
No cohort is frozen here: a separately reviewed future caller can rank eligible
TRAIN/VAL identities by a new fixed namespace and freeze exactly32 FIT/16 CAL
**before HTTP**, without substitution or availability filtering. If either
minimum fails, it closes before RGB/model/FIT. Existing TEST/pilot RESERVED
references remain unopened; no candidate, parameter or gate is chosen on them.

Publisher CC-BY2 photograph metadata and inherited V-COCO source notices are
private-research provenance, not independently verified creator rights or a
submission grant. Creator identity is UNKNOWN: photo disjointness does not prove
author independence. Model exposure to COCO/V-COCO may exist; exact overlap is
unverified. Published positive general roles are not hand ownership, contact,
task-target, full negative labels, temporal identity or 3D truth. Tiny authored
streams qualify mechanics only, not measured capacity, FIT quality or CARI4D.

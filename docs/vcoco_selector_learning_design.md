# Coherent foreground relation learner — prospective design, not a frozen FIT

Independent method audit, October6. No FIT references, fitted weights, once-only
CAL scores or quality adoption are available. Freeze the execution budget and
this scientific recipe **before** reading fresh FIT role values. Mechanical

## Why selection precedes tracking

SAM concept masks and long-lived tracks can consistently describe a bystander.
Keep complete automatic person/left-right/object hypotheses and assemble each
native relation's geometry and logits before marginalizing. Never independently
take the best wrist distance, box overlap and logit from different candidates.
The existing exact hierarchical prior balances supported sides, distinct full
geometries and native margins; aliases do not gain mass through duplication.
Missing anatomy stays raw NaN with numerical-support indicators. Empty HOI is
missing relation evidence, not OFF/no-contact or a deleted object bank.

This is an incomplete-positive-set MIL surrogate, not certified negatives or

## Once-only CAL and honest scope

Source-native unique(person-annotation,object-annotation) pairs receive one mass;
duplicate action/role rows do not amplify loss. Keep **all** published positives,
including detector misses. Joint matching requires personIoU>=.5 **and**
objectIoU>=.5. A positive-mask tensor with no match must not hide an absent GT
pair: the objective's status alone does not certify reference coverage.

Proposed primary CAL: fixed16 macro one-to-one pairRecall@3. Secondary
any-positiveRecall@1 and Recall@1/5, complete paired A/B differences, coverage
ceilings and conditional retrieval. Rank budgets are metric views, never raw
bank deletion. Exact sign comparisons are descriptive here; CAL is evaluated
once after artifact/config freeze, not for tuning, adoption or a final held-out
victory claim. A new independent temporal/ownership/full-T3D validation remains
necessary before using the selector to seed reconstruction.

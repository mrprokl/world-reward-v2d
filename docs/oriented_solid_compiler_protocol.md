# Whole-oriented-solid compiler: prepared, not qualified

This branch extends the general representation contract to multiple cavities,
islands and independent roots. It does not change a prediction, select an
episode, repair a surface or claim improved reconstruction accuracy.

`infra/oriented_solid_compiler.py` surrounds the unchanged cached QEM with
certificates on six complete representations: physical source, native candidate,
F32 GLB, actual default-eight-digit Trimesh processing, unmodified official 4096
packing and once-only metric grounding. Original face/vertex births establish a
bijective full oriented-solid forest at every stage. Existing 1% Chamfer and 5%
per-shell/net-volume limits remain fixed. The caller must authenticate actual
qualified binaries, their licenses, image and the official helper; mock tests
are not native qualification. All components, original oriented faces and
referenced vertices remain in the source contract.

## Frozen v1 controls: rejected before QEM

The four procedural curved sources are generated in
`infra/oriented_solid_controls.py`; their complete typed-array hashes and
measured source-chart diagnostics are frozen in
`configs/oriented_solid_controls_v1_manifest.json`.

On 2026-10-04 the all-source chart gate is **FAIL**. The third source,
`newforest_island_independentroot_identity`, uses midpoint origin `(0.625,0,0)`
and dyadic scale `4`. Encoding/decoding changes 1477 coordinates with maximum
error `2^-53`. The other three source roundtrips pass. Zero QEM/CGAL calls were
made for this cohort; its expected forest is not a stored-geometry certificate.

Preserve this rejection. Do not reroll fixtures, snap coordinates, drop a source,
relax exactness, reinterpret the manifest or run known-failing qualification.
The regression test passing means the failure is detected, not that the compiler
passes. A different chart is a separately source-bound numerical method requiring
a fresh predeclared cohort and the same downstream geometric gates.

The next deterministic hypothesis is per-axis translation certified by
[Sterbenz's lemma (Goldberg 1991, theorem 11)](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/E19957-01/806-3568/ncg_goldberg.html),
otherwise origin zero, followed by dyadic `ldexp`. Chart selection must precede
QEM and cannot retry origins according to observed performance. Complete-source
range and inverse-exactness remain mandatory. This is a conditioning hypothesis,
not a guarantee of QEM quality, metric identifiability or HOI accuracy.

on its closed COCO64 study. CARI4D's concept-mask union, 4DAnyone's summed track
area and our historical confident-object-first selector do not establish the
interacting person and object. The next discriminant is retrieval of an original
annotated `holds` person–object pair among competing people and objects. Hand
sides remain latent; a published positive does not identify an exclusive task
target, physical contact or an exhaustive negative/OFF set.

Use cached original OpenImages **TEST** bbox/relations/triplets/image metadata,
not old predictions or an alternative seed of any closed cohort. All historical

study is photo-disjoint and publisher-author-disjoint where identities exist,
not certified subject/event/pretraining-disjoint. OWLv2 has dataset-level
OpenImages overlap; exact training-photo overlap remains unknown.

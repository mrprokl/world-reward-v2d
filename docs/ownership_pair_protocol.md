annotated `holds` person–object pair among competing people and objects. Hand
sides remain latent; a published positive does not identify an exclusive task
target, physical contact or an exhaustive negative/OFF set.

Use cached original OpenImages **TEST** bbox/relations/triplets/image metadata,
not old predictions or an alternative seed of any closed cohort. All historical

study is photo-disjoint and publisher-author-disjoint where identities exist,
not certified subject/event/pretraining-disjoint. OWLv2 has dataset-level

and native HOI-DETR routes. RGB only, shared bank/support/geometric coefficients
for both arms; B alone adds learned relational margin evidence. No topK deletion

indicator-only owner without geometry. It does not itself learn/select/calibrate.

mechanical checks, before their corresponding reference values are consumed.

winner means failure to retrieve a published positive, not a true false-contact
label. Every original slot contributes. A gain here can qualify this narrow
static mechanism only. Adoption still requires stationary targets, bystanders,
occlusion/release and full-original-T shared-frame reconstruction non-regression.
No static component PASS, overlay, engineering pack or proxy improvement proves
a CARI4D/leaderboard victory.
